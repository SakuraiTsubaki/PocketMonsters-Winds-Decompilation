# Press and Media Assets — Public-Source Inventory

**Research date:** 2026-09-14  
**Target repository:** Pocket Monsters Winds

This document inventories official public-facing media and press surfaces connected to Pocket Monsters Winds / Pocket Monsters Waves. It does **not** treat public website asset names as internal game asset names, archive paths, symbols, or executable identifiers.

## The Pokémon Company International press surfaces

The official TPCi press site currently exposes indexed Winds/Waves material including:

- a 2026-02-27 announcement press release;
- a dedicated **Pokémon Winds and Pokémon Waves** press-assets hub;
- a **Pokémon Day 2026** assets hub that references the titles;
- the TPCi product schedule listing both titles for Nintendo Switch 2 in 2027.

The canonical indexed URLs are recorded in `../../manifests/public-sources-batch-0003.json`.

During the current census pass, direct automated access to several TPCi press pages returned HTTP 401. Their state is therefore recorded as `indexed-access-restricted`, not as fully reviewed. Individual artwork/video filenames from those restricted pages must not be guessed.

## Official product-site galleries

The checked US official product landing page exposes several distinct public image groups:

- 12 images associated with the world/open-world presentation;
- 8 images associated with the protagonist/version presentation;
- 5 images associated with the first-partner presentation.

Gallery count and accessibility labels are public-site presentation metadata. They do not establish how the retail game stores or names the corresponding assets.

Nintendo of America-operated Latin-American storefronts checked expose a 7-image product carousel for individual titles. These storefront galleries are tracked per regional surface because image order, labels, availability, and future replacements may differ.

## Directly connected official partner media

The Winds/Waves main theme is also represented through official NHK Symphony Orchestra / Pokémon campaign material. The Pokémon campaign page and the four NHK Symphony Orchestra concert pages for Tokyo, Kyoto, Osaka, and Fukuoka are registered in `../../manifests/public-sources-batch-0003.json`.

These partner pages are in scope because they directly identify and perform the Winds/Waves main theme. Unrelated Pokémon concert material is out of scope unless it directly connects to Generation X.

## Asset handling rule

Publicly visible screenshots, artwork, logos, videos, accessibility labels, filenames, and page metadata are all researchable evidence. Before storing an original media file in this repository, redistribution status and project need must be considered separately. Source URLs, identifiers, metadata, hashes of lawfully obtained research copies where appropriate, derived analysis, comparison data, and project-created documentation are preferred for provenance.

If a public image or video later changes, the earlier observed state should be preserved in the change history rather than silently overwritten.
